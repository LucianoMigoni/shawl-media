Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

"Shawl Videos" is an internal tool used only by the Shawl Games team. It publishes the team's own videos to the team's own YouTube channel and reads that channel's statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Upload access to the Shawl Games YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload), to publish our videos.
  • Read-only access to that channel and its videos (scope youtube.readonly), to check that a video was published.
  • Read-only access to that channel's YouTube Analytics reports (scope yt-analytics.readonly), to measure how our videos perform.

How it is used and stored

The authorization token is stored only on the team's own computers, as an environment variable. Channel statistics are saved in the team's private project files. We do not collect data about viewers or any other users, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Shawl Videos' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
